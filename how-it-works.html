How VIDYA AI works

The books and the app both live on the phone.

The regular VIDYA AI app looks in the books saved on the phone. It writes the answer on the phone too. It does not send your questions over the internet.

See how to set it up

Setup · before your first question

First, the app and the books are put on the phone.

First, the app, the AI file and your books must be saved on the phone. Contact us about setup. For now, the files are copied to the phone with a USB cable.

These files need space on the phone. The AI file is the part that writes answers. That file alone is about 806 MB. The total space depends on the app version and the books. Before setup, check that the phone can run the app and has enough free space.

What happens when you ask

Then, ask a question about your textbook.

Scroll down to see the four things that happen on the phone after you press send.

It looks for the part of the book that can help.

VIDYA AI starts with the books for your class. If another class's book looks much more likely to have the answer, it may look there too.

It uses that part of the book to write an answer.

The AI works on the phone itself. The regular app does not need internet or mobile data for this.

It shows which part of the book it looked in.

A label under the answer shows where it looked. It does not show the exact page yet. The label also does not show when you open an old chat again.

It keeps your chats on the phone.

You can go back to recent chats or delete them on the phone.

What it cannot do well

Looking in the book helps, but answers can still be wrong.

VIDYA AI can look in the wrong part of a book. It can miss an important fact. If it cannot find enough help in the books, it should say so. Even so, some answers can be wrong.

We tested on one expensive, powerful phone. There, a chapter took about 42–80 seconds to open for the first time. Starting the app after it was fully closed can take two to three minutes. Other phones may be faster or slower.

Good to know

Studying without internet and getting updates online are different.

Today, the regular app gets its books when the files are copied to the phone with a USB cable. Downloading books inside the app is only in a separate test version that can go online. It is not in the regular student app yet.

Later versions may add online features. Study help would still work without internet. These are plans, not things you can use today.
