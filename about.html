About us

Study help should be there when the question comes.

A child's question does not wait for a strong signal, a new data pack or the next school day. VIDYA AI helps with textbook questions on the student's Android phone. It needs no internet once it is set up.

What we chose

You can see our choices in the app.

Four choices you can check in the app yourself, not just read about.

1

Start with the book the child is studying.

Before it writes an answer, the app looks in the school books saved on the phone.

2

Make it work without internet.

The regular app runs on the phone. It is not allowed to use the internet. Setup takes some work, but daily questions need no internet.

3

Take care with children.

The regular app keeps chats on the phone. It does not push children to use it every day. Automatic answer checking stays off until tests show it is right often enough.

4

Be honest about what works.

We say which features work now and which are only being tested. Our test results do not prove better exam marks. We clearly mark future plans.

Test results

We test the app, and we tell you what it gets wrong.

Our test reports cover phone tests of the student app, how it looks in the books, and a separate test version that can go online.

The tests also show what needs work. In one phone test, answers had the main fact we checked for in 74.5% of English cases and 58.3% of Marathi cases. These numbers are for that test only. They do not tell you how often the app will be right everywhere. They do not mean students got better marks.

We have not proved that VIDYA AI improves learning or exam marks. We also need more tests on different low-cost phones.
