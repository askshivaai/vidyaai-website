The basics

What is VIDYA AI?
VIDYA AI helps Maharashtra State Board students in Classes 5 to 10 with textbook questions on an Android phone. It looks in the books on the phone, then writes an answer.
Who is it for?
It is for students who use the Balbharati books in the app, and their parents. It is also for schools that want a small trial. Teachers can check if it suits their classes.
Does it need internet?
No. Once the app and books are on the phone, it answers without internet. The regular app is not allowed to use the internet. For now, you copy the files over with a USB cable. A separate test version can go online.
Does VIDYA AI replace school or tuition?
No. VIDYA AI is extra study help. It does not replace teachers. It makes no promise about marks, and it cannot meet every study need.

Books and languages

Is every textbook covered?
No. There are 33 sets of textbook files for Classes 5 to 10, in English and Marathi. 19 sets also have Hindi. Some books are missing or need fixes. Check your book before setup.
Is it available for CBSE, ICSE or every state board?
It is made for Maharashtra State Board books. We have not confirmed any other boards.
Can my child ask in Marathi, Hindi or Hinglish?
Yes. But some answers may be unclear or in the wrong language. If you choose Marathi, the menus are still in English. Try your language first before you rely on it.

Answers and what it can do

Are all answers correct?
No. It can miss facts, pick the wrong chapter, or give a wrong or unclear answer. Check important answers in your textbook or with a teacher.
What does “Looked in your book” mean?
It is the book label. It shows which part of the book it looked in. It does not prove the answer is right. It does not show the page number yet.
Why can the first answer take time?
The app needs time to open the book and get the AI file ready. On the test phone, a new chapter could take about a minute or longer. Starting the app after it was fully closed could take two to three minutes. Other phones may differ.
Can students speak their questions?
Not in the regular app. Speaking questions is planned.
Can it read homework photos or handwritten papers?
Not now. Reading homework photos is planned.
Can it solve maths step by step?
Not yet. A step-by-step maths tool is planned. Check your sums and methods with a teacher.
Does it automatically test or mark my child?
No. Practice questions and answer checking are turned off in the regular app.

Privacy and study logs

Will I get a parent progress report?
Not yet. Reports for parents are being built. There is no report page or service you can use yet.
Are study chats uploaded?
No. The regular app keeps chats on the phone and is not allowed to use the internet. A separate test version can send a small study log after a parent says yes. It has no questions, answers or chats.
Can my child delete chats?
Yes. Chats can be deleted, and Settings can clear all chats. If you join a trial of the test version, you should first be told how its records are used and deleted.

How to get it and cost

Is it free?
The current student app is described as free to use. After setup, answering uses no mobile data. We have not shared prices for setup help, school trials or future services.
Can I install it from the Play Store?
Not yet. Contact us to ask about the books you need, whether it works on your phone and how to set it up.
Does it work on every Android phone?
We do not know yet. Space, memory and speed must be checked on your phone. Testing on low-cost phones is not finished.
Is there an iPhone version?
We have not confirmed an iPhone version. The current app is for Android phones.
Can a school start using it for all students immediately?
No. First we talk about which students, books and phones, and how to keep their information private. The school version is still a separate test version. It is not ready for a whole school.
