How it works

Ask a question in three simple steps.

All on the phone · no internet needed

1

Type your question.

Type the part you do not understand.

2

It looks in your books.

It searches the books saved on the phone, starting with your class.

3

Read the answer, then check.

A label shows which part of the book it looked in. Keep your textbook close to check.

Give it time. Opening a chapter for the first time can take about a minute or more. Starting the app after it was fully closed can take two to three minutes. It is not an instant-answer app.

Why it helps

Made for the way a child really studies.

It starts with the textbook. It keeps working without internet. It treats your child with care.

Answer written on the phone

Starts with the school book

Before it answers, VIDYA AI looks in the Balbharati books in the app. It starts with the book you are studying.

Works without internet

The regular app is not allowed to use the internet. Once the app and books are on the phone, it writes answers on the phone itself.

Chats stay on the phone

In the regular app, your child's name and chats stay on the phone.

Calm study, no pressure

Pick a friendly study buddy. Ask a question. Go back to an old chat. There are no daily rewards and no alerts asking your child to come back.

Books and languages

Start with the books your child uses.

VIDYA AI is for Maharashtra State Board students in Classes 5–10. You can ask for answers in English, Marathi, Hindi or Hinglish (Hindi typed in English letters). Some answers may be unclear or come in a different language. We tell you about these limits before you start.

What works and what does not yet

What works now, and what still needs work.

VIDYA AI is an early version. Its answers can be wrong. It can miss a fact, pick the wrong chapter or give an unclear answer. Marathi, Hindi and Hinglish answers still need work.

Each answer has a book label that shows which part of the book it looked in. We tell you what still needs work. The part that checks students' answers is turned off. It needs more testing.

Quick questions

Three things people ask first.

Does VIDYA AI need internet every day?
No. Once the app, the AI file (the part that writes answers) and the books are on the phone, the regular app answers without internet.
Can it replace a teacher or tuition?
No. VIDYA AI is extra study help. It does not replace a teacher. We make no promise about marks or results.
Can I download it now?
Not yet. It is not on the Play Store yet. Contact us to ask how to get the early version.
