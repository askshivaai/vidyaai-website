VIDYA AI for schools

Study help that works at home, even without internet.

VIDYA AI is being built to help students with questions about their textbooks at home. It needs no internet once it is set up. Start by checking your school books. Then try the app on the Android phones families use.

Talk about a school trialCheck books and languages

Please note: We can talk about a small school trial. This does not mean the app is ready for every student in your school.

Questions schools ask

Start with the questions that matter to your school.

Does it match our textbooks?

For now it is for Maharashtra State Board Classes 5–10. Before any trial, we check together each class and book you want to use.

Will it work on the phones families have?

The phone needs enough free space and must be able to run the app. We have not finished testing on low-cost phones. Try it on the phones families really use.

Can children study when the internet is poor?

Yes. Once the app and its books are set up, the regular student app writes answers without internet.

Do teachers still have a part?

Yes. VIDYA AI is extra study help, not a teacher. Teachers should read some answers and check that they match the school books.

What happens to student data?

The regular app works without internet and keeps chats on the phone. A separate test version that can go online works differently. We explain it below.

Steps for a small trial

Start small, and learn what works.

A trial is a way to learn. It is not a full launch. These five steps keep it small and honest.

Look back at it together

1

Decide what to try

Choose the classes, books, language and phones you want to try.

2

Check that everyone is ready

Before students use it, check the phones and the setup. Explain the trial to parents. Check the rules for keeping children's information private.

3

Try real questions

Teachers check if the answers are helpful, correct and about the right textbook.

4

Look back at it together

Check how long answers take and if the language is clear. Note which answers are wrong and where students still need a teacher.

5

Decide the next step

Use what you learned to decide: carry on, try with fewer topics or students, or wait until the app is better.

Separate test version

Linking to a school is only in a separate test version.

A separate test version can go online. It can link a student's phone to a school with a code. It asks a parent for permission before it shares a small study log. It can also download book files and check them before use. It has been tested on a phone. It is not the regular student app, and it is not ready for schools.

The study log has only codes, numbers and times. It has no names, email addresses, questions, answers or chats. The app sends a school code once, when it first links the phone to the school. That code is not sent with later study logs.

In this test version, a parent must say yes before any study log is shared. A lawyer still needs to check the rules. More work is needed before a school trial.

Reports for parents and teachers, study plans based on exam dates and links to school software are being built or planned. Schools cannot use them yet.

What the test version sends after a parent says yes

Sent

  • A small study log (codes, numbers, times)
  • A few settings
  • School code once, when first linking

Never sent with the log

  • Child's name
  • Email address
  • Questions and answers
  • Chats

What to check in a trial

Check how useful it is when students study.

When you try the app, we suggest you check:

  • Did it look in the right book and chapter?
  • Does the answer have the facts it needs?
  • Can students understand the language of the answer?
  • Is it quick enough on the phones in the trial?
  • Do families understand the setup and privacy choices?
  • Where do students still need a teacher's help?

These are things to check in a trial. We do not promise better marks, and nothing has proved that yet.
