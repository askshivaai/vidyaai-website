Privacy and safety

We take care with a child's study questions.

The regular VIDYA AI app keeps study chats on the phone and works without internet. A separate test version can go online. We explain it on its own below, so you can see the difference.

See how to get the app
Regular app

What the regular app does

The app is not allowed to use the internet.

Android does not let this app go online. It answers questions on the phone itself.

The child's name and chats stay on the phone.

The regular app saves them on the phone. It does not send them to us. Anyone who opens the unlocked phone may still read the saved chats.

A small study log does not include chats.

The app keeps a small study log, such as which chapter was used and when. It uses only codes, numbers and times. It has no names, questions or answers. Chats are saved separately on the phone.

The student can clear chat history.

You can clear chats in Settings. In the regular app today, this also clears the study log on the phone. For the separate test version, we have not yet decided the rules for deleting logs and logs waiting to be sent.

No pressure

No pressure to keep coming back.

The app has no daily rewards, points, timers, badges or alerts. A friendly study buddy keeps it welcoming. Using it every day is not a competition.

Automatic answer checking is turned off in the regular app. The AI does not mark a child's answer as wrong.

Separate test version

The separate test version has different rules.

This separate test version can go online. It can link a phone to a school and download book files. A parent or guardian must say yes before it shares any study log.

After a parent says yes, it can send a small study log and a few settings. It does not send the child's name, email, questions, answers or chats. A school code is sent once when linking the phone, not with later logs.

This version is still being tested. A lawyer must check the rules before real student information is used. It is not ready for schools yet.

This website

This website asks only for what a message needs.

Your first message does not need a child's name, school record or saved chat.

In this version, this site sets no cookies and loads no tracking tools from other companies. The contact page writes a message for you to copy or send from your own email app. This site does not store it.

If we add forms or visitor-counting tools later, we will update this page first.

Legal checks

Families should know how their information is handled.

We are writing the rules for student information in the separate test version. A lawyer still needs to check them. We do not claim that all legal checks and approvals are done.

Note: we still need a proper legal privacy notice. It must name the company and a contact person. It must say why information is used, how long it is kept and what rights people have. This page explains privacy in simple words. It does not replace that notice.
