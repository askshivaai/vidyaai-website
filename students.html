VIDYA AI for students

Stuck on a textbook topic? Start with your question.

Your question does not need to be perfect. Type the part you do not understand. VIDYA AI looks for help in the school books saved in the app.

For Maharashtra State Board students in Classes 5–10. This is an early version. Contact us to ask how to set it up.

When you can use it

A place to ask when you study on your own.

After class

Go back to a topic you did not fully understand.

During homework

Ask about a topic before you write your answer. The book must be in the app.

While revising

Ask for an explanation or the main points. Then check the reply with your book.

When there is no internet

Keep asking questions. The app searches the books on the phone, without internet.

What you can do

Make it your own study space.

Small choices that make the app feel like yours. We write the limits next to each one.

1. Choose a study buddy

Pick a character you like. Your buddy sets the app's colours. It moves while VIDYA AI is working.

2. Ask in your own words

Type your question. Not sure what to ask? Suggested questions can help you begin.

3. See where VIDYA AI looked

Under a new reply, the book label shows the class, subject and chapter it looked in. The answer can still be wrong.

4. Go back to an old chat

Recent chats stay on the phone so you can read them again or delete them.

5. Carry on from where you stopped

Still being checked on more phones

VIDYA AI can show the chapter you studied last. It can also show a small reminder in the app to revise. We are still checking these new parts on different phones.

Example questions

What you can ask

  • What is a food chain?
  • Explain physical and chemical changes simply.
  • प्रकाशसंश्लेषण म्हणजे काय?
  • इस अध्याय के मुख्य बिंदु क्या हैं?

These examples show how you can ask. To help, the app needs the right book and must find the right part of it. Some Hindi questions may get a reply in another language.

What to keep in mind

Check important answers with your book or teacher.

Read the reply carefully. If something looks wrong, check it in your textbook or ask a teacher.

For now, you need to type your questions. The regular app cannot yet take spoken questions, read photos of homework, give marks, or solve maths step by step with a special tool.

  • Spoken questions · not yet
  • Homework photos · not yet
  • Marks or grades · not yet
  • Step-by-step maths tool · not yet
