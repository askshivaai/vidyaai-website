VIDYA AI for parents and guardians

You do not need to know every answer to help your child learn.

You may be busy, not know a chapter, or prefer another language. VIDYA AI gives your child another place to ask textbook questions. It works on an Android phone, without internet once it is set up.

How it helps

Help when you cannot sit with your child to study.

VIDYA AI can help your child go back to a school topic, ask about a confusing part and read a reply based on books available in the app.

It is made to help with study at home, alongside school. It does not promise higher marks. It does not replace what a teacher decides.

A typical evening

  1. 7:40 pm

    Homework starts. One line in the Science chapter is confusing.

  2. 7:42 pm

    Your child types the question. The phone has no internet tonight.

  3. 7:44 pm

    VIDYA AI looks in the Class 7 Science book saved on the phone and writes a reply.

  4. 7:45 pm

    The book label shows which chapter it looked in. Your child checks the textbook before writing.

This is an example of how it could be used, not a real session. Times are examples. Opening a chapter for the first time can take about a minute or longer.

Privacy and calm

A study space with fewer reasons to worry.

Chats stay on the phone

The regular app saves your child's name and chats on the phone. The app is not allowed to use the internet.

No pressure to use the app every day

The student app does not give rewards for daily use. It does not send alerts asking your child to come back. Your child can use it when they need help.

It does not mark your child's answers

The part that checks a student's answer is turned off in the regular app. VIDYA AI does not let its current AI decide that a child's answer is wrong.

Clear limits before you begin

We tell you which books are included, which languages need more work, and how long a chapter may take to open. This helps you decide if the app suits your child.

What to expect

Know what the app can and cannot do.

VIDYA AI can make mistakes. Check important definitions, sums and exam answers in the textbook or with a teacher.

In tests so far, English answers included the main fact more often than some answers in other languages. Marathi, Hindi and Hinglish (Hindi typed in English letters) still need work. A new chapter may take time to open.

We are building reports to help parents see their child's study progress. A page for parents to see these reports is not ready yet.
