When it helps

When VIDYA AI is a good fit, when it is not, and what to check first.

Good fit

  • A Maharashtra State Board student in Classes 5 to 10 revising a chapter at home. They can ask what a word means or for the main points of a topic, if the book is in the app.
  • A home where the internet often stops or the data pack is small. Once set up, the app keeps working without internet.
  • A parent who wants one more place for their child to ask textbook questions. Chats stay on the phone, and nothing pushes the child to use it every day.
  • A school that wants a small trial, checked by teachers, with a few students using the Android phones their families already have.

Not a fit, or not yet

  • Students on other boards, or not in Classes 5 to 10. Other boards are not set up in this version.
  • Subjects or textbooks that are not in the app yet. Not every book is included, so check your exact book.
  • Anyone who needs to ask by voice, read a homework photo, get automatic marking, a step-by-step maths tool, or a Play Store install. These are turned off or planned.
  • Anyone who needs an answer right away. On the phone we tested, opening a chapter for the first time took 42 to 80 seconds.
  • A school that wants ready-made reports. Summaries for parents and teachers are being built in a separate test version. It still needs a legal check.
  • iPhone users. The product information we have does not confirm an iPhone version.

What to check before you ask for the app

  1. Class and board: Maharashtra State Board, Classes 5 to 10.
  2. Textbook names and school language: English, Marathi, Hindi or Hinglish (Hindi typed in English letters). Each has known limits.
  3. The child's own Android phone. It needs space on the phone for the AI file (about 806 MB) plus books, and enough memory to run the app. Tests on low-cost phones are not finished.
  4. Setup: we help you copy the files to the phone with a USB cable, once.
