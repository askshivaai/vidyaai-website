Comparing it fairly

We do not rank VIDYA AI against other products. We do not say it is the first, only or best. These notes show how it is different, so you can compare fairly.

Compared with an online chatbot

  • Where answers come from. VIDYA AI looks in the Balbharati book files on the phone before it answers. It shows which chapter it looked in. An online chatbot answers from general knowledge and may not know your books and chapters.
  • Internet. The regular VIDYA AI app is not allowed to use the internet. It works without internet once set up. An online chatbot needs internet for every question.
  • Your chats. The regular VIDYA AI app keeps chats on the phone. An online chatbot sends every question to a computer somewhere else.
  • Speed and topics. An online chatbot is usually faster and knows far more topics. VIDYA AI is slower the first time it opens a chapter. It only knows the books in the app.

Compared with a tuition or video-lesson app

  • VIDYA AI does not give lessons, live teachers, tests or marks. It answers typed questions about the textbook.
  • It has no streaks, points, badges or alerts asking you to come back. Many lesson apps use these.
  • It is described as free to use now. Prices for school trials and online services have not been announced.

Compared with the textbook alone

  • If the app and the textbook disagree, trust the textbook. VIDYA AI can explain part of it in simpler words or another language. It can also be wrong.
  • Check a VIDYA AI answer in the book. The book label shows which part of the book to look in.

What a fair comparison should not do

  • Treat one test of whether answers included the right facts (74.5% English, 58.3% Marathi) as a score for how often answers are right in general.
  • Assume VIDYA AI improves marks. No study has shown this.
  • Mix up the regular app, which works without internet, with the separate test version that can go online. They have different rules.
